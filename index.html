My story

How I Got Here

I spent twenty years in international operations management before I wrote my first book. Then, in 2012, my husband faced a serious medical crisis, and I sat beside him and wrote his story so our children would have something true to hold on to. That book, God Was Holding My Hand, taught me what writing actually does: it gives form to the things people most need to say.

What started as one story became a vocation. I realized that helping someone articulate their experience, finding the precise words for something they've lived through but never quite been able to say, was the most meaningful work I could imagine.

"The best books aren't written alone. They're built through honest conversation, patient listening, and a deep respect for the person behind the ideas."

Today I run a small, intentional practice. Not an agency. A partner. Every manuscript I touch gets my full attention, from the first interview to the final page. Because the authors I work with have spent years earning the expertise that belongs inside this book, and they deserve a writer who treats it that way.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to write a book?
Most ghostwriting projects take 6 to 12 months depending on scope and complexity. Some shorter books or faster-paced projects can be completed in 3 to 4 months. We'll map out a realistic timeline in your custom proposal.
How much does ghostwriting cost?
Investment varies based on book length, research required, and timeline. Every project gets a custom proposal after our discovery call. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here.
Will the book sound like me?
Absolutely. Capturing your authentic voice is the most important part of the process. I study how you speak, read your existing writing, and interview you extensively before writing a single word.
Is ghostwriting ethical?
Yes, and it's more common than most people realize. Many bestselling business books, memoirs, and leadership titles are written with a ghostwriter. The ideas, expertise, and story are entirely yours. I'm here to help you say it well.
Will anyone know you wrote my book?
Only you. Confidentiality is a non-negotiable part of how I work. Every project includes a non-disclosure agreement, and I take that commitment seriously.
What if I already have a draft?
Developmental editing might be the better fit. I can work with whatever you have, a rough draft, an outline, or just a collection of ideas, and help you shape it into a finished, publishable manuscript.
