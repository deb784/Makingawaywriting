Services

How Rachel helps you move from idea to finished book

Executive Ghostwriting

Interview led manuscript development for leaders, experts, founders, and professionals who need a book that matches the authority they already carry.

  • Voice matching and interview synthesis
  • Chapter development and manuscript drafting
  • Revision support through final delivery

Developmental Editing

Big picture editorial analysis for authors with a draft who need stronger structure, clearer pacing, and sharper positioning before line level polish.

  • Structural review and editorial letter
  • Chapter sequence and narrative flow analysis
  • Follow up strategy conversation

Book Strategy and Author Positioning

  • Reader and message clarity
  • Book outline and concept refinement
  • Publishing path and platform guidance

About Rachel

Two decades in business. One book at a time.

Rachel Arterberry is a professional ghostwriter and developmental editor based in New Fairfield, Connecticut. She works with coaches, consultants, executives, medical and financial professionals, nonprofit founders, and thought leaders who want a book that reflects the depth of the work they have already done.

Her practice centers on confidentiality, strong editorial structure, and voice preservation. The work is personal, strategic, and built for authors who need both clarity and momentum.
